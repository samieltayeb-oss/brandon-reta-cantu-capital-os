## 1. Bottom line (H)
A signed first deal in 30 days is possible but unlikely from pure cold outreach by a foreign vendor with no case study. The realistic path is a small, fixed-price, remotely delivered pilot sold to an owner-run Saudi SME (or a Saudi subsidiary of a firm with a Western link), ideally through a warm referral or local partner, paid 100% (or 50%+) upfront by wire in SAR. Plan for "signed pilot" as the 30-day goal and a CA$15K+ project as the 60 to 90 day goal.

## 2. Entry offer: "Connected Workflow Pilot" (PENDING CEO APPROVAL)
- Scope (H): one workflow (e.g. WhatsApp/web enquiry to CRM to quote/follow-up), built on the client's existing tools or a light CRM, Arabic/English messages, handover doc and 30-minute training. 3 weeks remote delivery. No access to sensitive personal data beyond what the workflow needs; data stays in client-owned accounts (see PDPL, s.5).
- Front door: keep the CEO-approved FREE 20-minute intro and FREE focused assessment (GTM-32 policy: 60 to 90 min, max 2 workflows, no ROI claims). The pilot is the first paid step.
- Proposed price: SAR 19,500 fixed, excl. VAT (PENDING CEO APPROVAL).
  - Basis: CA$7,500 midpoint of the unapproved Automation & CRM Sprint range (CA$3K to 9K, GTM-29 B_commercial_offers.md) skewed up for foreign delivery, Arabic copy and FX/wire costs (H).
  - Conversion: CA$7,500 x 2.6777 SAR/CAD = SAR 20,083, rounded down to SAR 19,500 for a clean, below-20K figure (H). Rate V: open.er-api.com CAD/SAR 2.677734, last update Sat 24 Oct 2026 00:02 UTC (Fri 23 Oct 2026 18:02 MDT). SAR is pegged to USD at 3.75, so CAD/SAR moves with CAD/USD (V, long-standing SAMA peg).
  - Cost basis: UNKNOWN (no NEXORA rate card, hours or margin target yet). Needed: hourly cost per role, past sprint hours, target margin, wire/FX fees.
  - Option: credit 100% of pilot fee to a follow-on build within 60 days (H, CEO decision).
- Lower alternative if the market resists (H): SAR 9,750 "Workflow Blueprint" (paid design only). Not recommended as default since it rarely converts to delivery value fast.

## 3. Channel mix (H unless noted)
1. Warm referrals and local partners (highest odds): Canadian/Saudi business links, Canadian Trade Commissioner Service in Riyadh (public service for Canadian exporters; CEO decision to engage, no contact made), Saudi Zoho/HubSpot/Odoo implementation partners who need overflow dev capacity, Saudi or Arab diaspora contacts of the team. A partner intro converts trust problems into a borrowed reputation.
2. LinkedIn: primary cold channel for decision makers in KSA; connect note first, value message after acceptance. Arabic or bilingual profile banner helps.
3. Email: formal, short, English first with Arabic version below for SMEs; send Sunday to Wednesday mornings.
4. WhatsApp: the default business channel in KSA, but use only after the contact has shared their number or replied (consent and courtesy norm; unsolicited WhatsApp is seen as intrusive and risks being blocked/reported). Use WhatsApp Business with a verified profile; voice notes are common, keep text short.
5. Language: decision makers in larger firms are comfortable in English; SME owners and ops managers respond better to Arabic. Arabic copy must be reviewed by a native speaker before use (UNKNOWN who: CEO decision).
6. Events: LEAP 2027 is 12 to 15 April 2027, Riyadh (V, onegiantleap.com, checked 24 Oct 2026), outside the 30-day window; useful for the 90+ day pipeline only.

## 4. Cadence (Sunday to Thursday)
- Workweek Sunday to Thursday; weekend Friday and Saturday (V, since 2013 Saudi change, general public knowledge).
- Riyadh is UTC+3; Calgary is UTC-6 now (UTC-7 after 1 Nov 2026), so 9 to 10 hours ahead. Best overlap: Calgary early morning (6 to 8 am MT) = Riyadh 3 to 5 pm; or schedule Riyadh 9 to 11 am messages the evening before (Calgary 11 pm to 1 am, use scheduled send).
- Prayer times: Dhuhr around noon to 12:30 pm and Asr around 3 pm Riyadh in late October/November (H, check a daily timetable); avoid booking calls in the 20 to 30 minutes after each adhan. Friday midday (Jumuah) is off limits.
- Sequence per prospect (H): Day 1 Sun LinkedIn connect; Day 2 Mon email; Day 4 Wed LinkedIn message if accepted; Day 8 Mon email follow-up; WhatsApp only after they give a number or reply; stop after 3 unanswered touches.
- Holidays in the window 25 Oct to 24 Nov 2026: no Saudi public holidays (V, Saudi official holidays are Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Founding Day 22 Feb and National Day 23 Sep). Note Founding Day 2027 falls inside Ramadan.
- Ramadan 2027 expected Mon 8 Feb to about 8 Mar 2027, Eid al-Fitr about 9 to 10 Mar 2027, moon sighting can shift a day (V, adatna.com updated 18 Oct 2026; niyatapp.com). Business slows and hours shorten; close follow-on deals before late January or after mid-March.
- Year end: many Saudi firms run calendar fiscal years, so December budget lock may help or block (H).

## 5. Trust builders and legal items
| Item | Finding | Label / source |
|---|---|---|
| Local registration | Selling cross-border remotely with no Saudi presence does not by itself require a Saudi entity (H). Any office, staff or local service delivery needs a MISA investment licence and Commercial Registration; get Saudi counsel before. | H; verify with counsel |
| Saudization / Nitaqat | Nitaqat quotas apply to employers in KSA. A remote Canadian vendor with no Saudi staff is not in scope (H). Matters only if NEXORA opens an entity. Message: "we support your team, we do not replace Saudi roles." | H |
| ZATCA FATOORA e-invoicing | Applies to persons registered for Saudi VAT. NEXORA as a non-resident without Saudi VAT registration does not issue FATOORA invoices (H). The Saudi buyer accounts for VAT via reverse charge on imported services. Issue a clear Canadian invoice (legal name, address, description, SAR amount). | H; verify with a Saudi tax adviser |
| VAT | Saudi standard VAT 15%; imported services to a VAT registered business are reverse charged by the customer (H from general knowledge, confirm on zatca.gov.sa). Quote "excl. VAT". | H |
| Withholding tax | Saudi WHT on payments to non-residents: 5% for technical and consulting services, 15% for royalties (V, ZATCA WHT General Guideline PDF; DLA Piper Feb 2024). Software licence/IP elements may be treated as royalties at 15%, so contracts should frame work as services with client-owned deliverables. Canada and KSA tax treaty: UNKNOWN (H: none in force); any credit in Canada for Saudi WHT needs NEXORA's accountant. Decide gross up or net. | V for rates, UNKNOWN for treaty |
| PDPL (SDAIA) | PDPL in force with SDAIA as regulator. Transfers of personal data outside KSA need an adequacy finding or safeguards such as SDAIA Standard Contractual Clauses, binding common rules or an accreditation certificate, plus a risk assessment (V, SDAIA Regulation on Personal Data Transfer Outside the Kingdom and SCC documents, sdaia.gov.sa, checked 24 Oct 2026). Design pilots so personal data stays in client owned systems (preferably KSA region hosting where offered) and NEXORA works through least privilege access; offer SDAIA SCCs in the contract. | V for rules, H for design |
| Other trust builders | Fixed scope and price, upfront acceptance criteria, weekly demo, client owns all code and accounts, Arabic UI/messages, a Saudi reference partner, Canadian company registration proof, references from Canadian pilots once verified. No case studies may be claimed yet (V, internal status). | H |

## 6. Payment terms (PENDING CEO APPROVAL)
- Pilot: 100% upfront preferred; fallback 60% on signing, 40% on acceptance within 5 business days of demo (H).
- Currency SAR (shows local commitment); accept USD at peg if the client prefers (H). Wire (SWIFT) to NEXORA's Canadian account; client pays its bank fees, NEXORA absorbs receiving fees (H). Bank details UNKNOWN.
- Prices excl. VAT; reverse charge by the client (H). WHT: state in contract whether price is gross (client withholds 5% and NEXORA nets 95%) or grossed up; recommendation: quote gross and accept 5% WHT for the pilot to reduce friction (H, CEO decision). Ask client for WHT certificate.
- Larger projects: 40/30/30 milestones, net 15 terms (H).

## 7. Week by week timeline (hypothesis)
- Week 1 (Sun 25 Oct to Thu 29 Oct): CEO approves offer, price, terms, Arabic reviewer. Pick 15 to 20 targets from KSA_LEADS_50 plus Scout's study; build bilingual one page pilot sheet, contract with PDPL SCC annex. Ask warm network for 3 to 5 intros.
- Week 2 (1 to 5 Nov): Outreach wave 1 (LinkedIn + email), partner conversations. Goal: 3 to 5 intro calls booked.
- Week 3 (8 to 12 Nov): Intro calls and free assessments (max 2 workflows). Goal: 2 pilot proposals sent within 48 hours of each assessment.
- Week 4 (15 to 19 Nov): Follow-up, procurement and WHT/VAT questions, contract redlines. Goal: 1 signed pilot and deposit wired.
- Buffer (22 to 24 Nov): signature and payment slippage. Payments by international wire can take several business days (H).
Odds (H, judgement, no statistics): signed pilot within 30 days roughly 10 to 20% with cold outreach only; roughly 25 to 40% if at least two warm intros or a local partner are in play; a CA$15K+ project in 30 days under 10%. Reasons: no case studies, foreign vendor, Saudi preference for relationships and in-person meetings, procurement and WHT paperwork, 9 to 10 hour time gap.
