## 0. Conflicts and corrections
- **K1. REG conflict.** REG row **P2** ("Any 'free assessment' / 'free audit' / 'free check' wording": **PROHIBITED**) conflicts with this journey. Proposed replacement rows are in §6.
  - The *paid* Connected Operations Assessment (Offer 1) is a different product. Its prices stay **PROHIBITED to quote** (REG P3).
  - The two must never share a name. **RECOMMENDATION:** call the free one "Free Limited Review (2-page summary)" or "Free Snapshot", not "Assessment".
- **K2. Citation correction.** Implied consent from an inquiry is **CASL s.10(10)(e)**, not s.10(10)(c)–(d). Verified text: "an inquiry or application, within the six-month period immediately before the day on which the message was sent, made by the person to whom the message is sent to any of those other persons, in respect of anything mentioned in any of paragraphs (a) to (c)." Paragraph (c) is bartering. Paragraph (d) is a written contract that is "currently in existence or expired within" the 2-year period.
  - **INTERPRETATION:** once the prospect accepts the free review, the existing business relationship may also arise under s.10(10)(b) ("acceptance… of a business… opportunity") or (d) (a written agreement for the review, if signed). Both carry a **2-year** window. That is uncertain for a free offer, so it goes to counsel. Until counsel answers, plan on the **6-month inquiry window**.
- **K3. CASL s.6(6) (verified).** "Paragraph (1)(a) does not apply to a commercial electronic message that **solely**":
  - "(a) provides a quote or estimate for the supply of a product… or a service… if the quote or estimate was requested by the person to whom the message is sent";
  - "(b) facilitates, completes or confirms a commercial transaction that the person… previously agreed to enter into with the person who sent the message".

  Paragraphs (c)–(e) and beyond cover warranty, subscription and employment matters and aren't relevant here.
  - **INTERPRETATION:**
    - Booking confirmations and delivering the requested 2-page summary plausibly fit (b), **if they contain nothing else**.
    - A requested paid proposal or quote plausibly fits (a).
    - Any added promotion (a newsletter, other services, "book a call to buy") breaks "solely".
  - s.6(6) removes only the **consent** requirement (s.6(1)(a)). The message must still contain the s.6(2) identification, contact information and unsubscribe (LEGAL REQUIREMENT, s.6(1)(b)).

## 1. Consent: offering and delivering the free review
- **L1. LEGAL REQUIREMENT.** s.6(1): no commercial electronic message without consent (express or implied) and s.6(2) content. s.13: the sender has the onus of proving consent.
- **I1. INTERPRETATION, by step.**
  - **Offering it (cold)** needs a pre-existing basis under CHK G2: s.10(9)(b) all three conditions, s.10(9)(c), a referral (SOR/2013-221 s.4(1)), or express consent. The free offer doesn't create consent by itself.
  - **The prospect books or requests:** this is an inquiry, so implied consent runs for **6 months from the request date** (s.10(10)(e)). Record the date.
  - **Confirmations, scheduling, delivering the summary:** possibly exempt from consent under s.6(6)(b) if solely transactional. The s.6(2) content is still required.
  - **Post-report promotional follow-up** relies on the s.10(10)(e) window, or express consent. Best practice is to ask for express consent at booking: an **unchecked**, separate box (CRTC Bulletin 2012-548 paras 16 and 18, INTERPRETATION), worded under s.10(1) and SOR/2012-36 s.4, including the withdrawal statement.
- **R1. RECOMMENDATION, consent record additions** (to CHK §5):
  - request date and channel;
  - **window expiry** (= request date + 6 months; recompute on each new inquiry);
  - express-consent checkbox state (Y/N, with wording version);
  - s.6(6) message log (confirmation and delivery messages tagged "transactional-only");
  - the date the summary was delivered;
  - the paid-proposal request date (s.6(6)(a)), if any.

## 2. Privacy and data minimization
- **L2. LEGAL REQUIREMENT, notice at booking.**
  - PIPA s.13(1): before or at collection, notify the person of "(a) … the purposes for which the information is collected, and (b)… the name or position name or title of a person who is able to answer… questions".
  - PIPA s.7(1): consent.
  - PIPA s.11(1): "only for purposes that are reasonable".
  - PIPEDA 4.2.3 and 4.4 where it applies.
- **L3. LEGAL REQUIREMENT, processors outside Canada.** PIPA s.13.1(1)–(3): where a service provider outside Canada collects or receives the information, notify "before or at the time" of:
  - (a) how to access the written policies on such providers;
  - (b) a contact person.

  This is in addition to s.13 (s.13.1(4)). PIPEDA 4.1.3: comparable protection by contract.
  - **INTERPRETATION:** likely triggers include booking tools (e.g. US-hosted schedulers), video platforms, CRMs and email platforms, and **AI note-takers or transcription tools**.
  - **RECOMMENDATION:**
    - no AI note-taker or recording without the prospect's **explicit agreement** at the start of the call;
    - list each tool and its hosting location in the privacy notice;
    - prefer Canadian-hosted tools where available.
- **L4. LEGAL REQUIREMENT, retention.** PIPA s.35(1): retain "only for as long as the organization reasonably requires… for legal or business purposes". s.35(2): then destroy or make non-identifying.
- **I2. INTERPRETATION.** A prospect's description of its operations is mostly business information. Names, contact details, and anything about employees, customers or health that slips in are personal information (PIPA s.1(1)(k)). The business-contact carve-outs (PIPA s.4(3)(d); PIPEDA s.4.01) are purpose-limited.
- **R2. RECOMMENDATION, minimization rules.**
  - **Booking form fields:** name, business email, company, role and an optional free-text "what would you like reviewed". No phone unless needed. No size or budget fields.
  - **Warning at booking and at the start of the call:** "Please don't share personal information about employees or customers, health information, passwords, or system access. This review uses only public information and what you choose to describe."
  - **Call notes:** business processes and tools only, with no individuals' details. If personal information is volunteered, don't record it, or redact it within 1 business day.
  - **2-page summary:** company-level only. No names other than the recipient contact. No employee, customer or health data. Marked "Prepared from public information and your description on [date]".
  - **Retention (proposed):**
    - notes and summary are kept **12 months** from delivery, or until the prospect asks to delete them;
    - if no proposal is requested, delete or anonymize them at the end of the window;
    - the suppression list is retained separately (CHK S5).
    - Counsel to confirm.
  - **No system access, logins, file uploads of customer data, or screenshares of live customer records.**
- **R3. RECOMMENDATION, clinic and HIA hold.** Clinic and health-sector prospects are not eligible for the free review until counsel confirms the HIA and PIPA scope (D20; PIPA s.4(3)(f)). If a non-clinic prospect starts describing patient or health records, stop and redirect.

## 3. Permissible contact procedures (booking and follow-up)
- **R4. RECOMMENDATION, procedure.**
  1. **Entry:** inbound request (website, event or referral) or an approved CHK-gated first message. No cold outreach without a recorded G2 basis.
  2. **Booking:** a NEXORA-domain form or link carrying the PIPA s.13 and s.13.1 notice, the minimization warning, the free-offer terms (§4), and an optional unchecked marketing consent box.
  3. **Confirmation and reminder:** transactional only, with s.6(2) content and the SIG.
  4. **Consultation (20 minutes):** state the terms orally (free, no obligation, limits). Note-taker consent, if any.
  5. **Free review delivery:** the 2-page summary by email, transactional only, with no added promotion (to stay within s.6(6)(b), INTERPRETATION).
  6. **Paid proposal:** only on request (s.6(6)(a)), and only with approved prices (D01). Never quote unapproved ranges (REG P3).
  7. **Support:** only after a signed contract.
- **I3. INTERPRETATION.** Phone follow-up isn't assessed (telemarketing and DNCL). Note: Competition Act s.52.1 applies to telemarketing (§4). LinkedIn and social DMs remain BLOCKED (CHK G11).

## 4. "Free" framing (Competition Act)
- **L5. LEGAL REQUIREMENT.**
  - s.74.01(1)(a) / s.52(1): no representation "false or misleading in a material respect".
  - s.52(4): "the general impression conveyed by a representation as well as its literal meaning shall be taken into account".
  - s.74.01(1)(c): no unapproved warranty or guarantee wording.
- **L6. Verification of free-offer provisions (verified search).** The Competition Act text has **no general provision on "free" offers** and no general clear-disclosure rule for them. The words "free", "without charge" and "disclaimer" do not appear. The only "no cost" provision is **s.52.1(3)(c)**, under **deceptive telemarketing** (oral communication "by any means of telecommunication", s.52.1(1)). It prohibits offering "a product at no cost… in consideration of the supply or use of another product, unless fair, reasonable and timely disclosure is made of the fair market value of the first product and of any restrictions, terms or conditions applicable to its supply". s.52.1(2)(b) also requires telemarketing disclosure of "any material restrictions, terms or conditions".
  - **INTERPRETATION:** if the free review is offered **by phone**, s.52.1 may apply (counsel). By email or web, general s.74.01(1)(a)/s.52(4) applies.
  - The drip-pricing provisions (verified text present in the Act, marginal note "Drip pricing"; exact subsection numbers to confirm) covers prices not attainable because of obligatory fees. That's relevant if any "free" item later carries a charge.
- **I4. INTERPRETATION (Competition Bureau guidance, not retrieved; labelled as guidance, UNVERIFIED).** The Bureau's published guidance on "free" and disclaimers is generally understood to expect conditions to be clear, prominent and close to the claim, and not to contradict the headline. This wasn't fetched or verified here. It should be confirmed by counsel or retrieved before reliance.
- **R5. RECOMMENDATION, accurate "free" terms** (shown on the booking page, in the confirmation, and orally):
  - "**Free 20-minute consultation** and a **free limited review**: no charge, no payment details, **no obligation** to buy. The review uses only public information and what you describe; NEXORA does not access your systems. You receive a 2-page summary. Detailed recommendations, design or implementation are paid services, quoted separately only if you ask."
  - **No hidden conditions:** no mandatory follow-up meeting, no auto-enrolment, no data-sharing condition, and no "free if you sign".
  - Don't call it an "assessment" or "audit" (K1). Don't state a "value" ("a $1,500 assessment, free") unless an approved price exists (D01; REG P3). Under s.52.1(3)(c) for phone, a fair-market-value disclosure could otherwise be required (counsel).
  - "**No obligation**" must be true in every respect: no fee, no exclusivity and no IP claim over what the prospect shares.
- **I5. INTERPRETATION, professional-advice risk.** A summary on systems or processes could be read as expert advice. Prospects might rely on it for decisions (e.g. security, privacy, accounting or legal points).
  - **RECOMMENDATION:** mark it "Preliminary observations from limited information. Not a full assessment, audit, security review, or legal, accounting or tax advice. Verify before acting."
  - Include no security-vulnerability claims, no compliance conclusions, and no outcome or savings estimates (s.74.01(1)(b)).
  - Counsel should confirm the liability and disclaimer wording (Legal Counsel Desk; D32).

## 5. Post-report follow-up cadence
- **L7. LEGAL REQUIREMENT.**
  - Implied consent from the inquiry lasts **6 months** from the request (s.10(10)(e)).
  - Each message needs the s.6(2) content and an unsubscribe valid **≥60 days** (s.11(2)), actioned **≤10 business days** (s.11(3)).
  - Check the suppression list before every send (CHK S4).
- **R6. RECOMMENDATION, proposed cadence** (stop at the first "no", unsubscribe or non-response limit):

| When | Message | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | Summary delivered (transactional only) | s.6(6)(b) (INTERPRETATION) + s.6(2) |
| Day 3–5 | One check-in: "Any questions on the summary? If useful, we can scope a paid proposal." | s.10(10)(e) |
| Day 14 | One value follow-up (no new claims; approved REG wording only) | s.10(10)(e) |
| Day 45 | Final check-in; offer to stay in touch **only with express consent** (ask for an opt-in) | s.10(10)(e) → seek s.10(1) |
| Before month 6 | **Stop** unless express consent was obtained, a new inquiry restarted the window, or a contract exists (s.10(10)(a)/(d), 2 years) | s.10(10) |

- Maximum of **3 promotional follow-ups** after delivery.
- Any reply of "not now" or "no" goes to the suppression list or a do-not-contact note.
- Verbal refusals count (CHK S2).
