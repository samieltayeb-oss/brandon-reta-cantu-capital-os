## Required fixes (section · current text → replacement)
1. **§4 "Price-objection line" (and §5 Part 3 "Brief changes").** Prospect-facing.
   - **Current:** "The call and the focused assessment are free; any implementation is proposed separately, priced from our approved schedule, and only if you ask."
   - **Replacement:** "The call and the [FREE REVIEW NAME] are free, with no obligation; any implementation is proposed separately in writing, only if you ask."
   - **Why:**
     - "focused assessment" pre-empts K-1 and falls under P2c if Option 2 is chosen.
     - "our approved schedule" asserts that a price schedule exists, but none does (D01). That makes it a s.74.01(1)(a) / s.52(4) accuracy risk (INTERPRETATION).
     - The "(Wording depends on the naming decision…)" note can then be deleted.
2. **§1 timing 15:00–18:00 and §1 0:00–2:00.** Prospect-facing spoken script.
   - **Current:** "explain the free review if useful" / "Free review and limits".
   - **Fix:** keep these. But make sure every spoken reference uses "[FREE REVIEW NAME]" and states the P2b limits verbatim:
     - "based only on public information and what you describe";
     - "no access to your systems";
     - "a 2-page summary";
     - "no obligation";
     - P2d: "Detailed recommendations, design and implementation are paid services, quoted separately on request."
   - **Why:** the limits must be disclosed clearly where the "free" claim is made (s.52(4), INTERPRETATION).
3. **§0 Naming table, row "Prohibited", Option 1 cell.**
   - **Current:** "REG P2 currently prohibits "free assessment" — would need to be SUPERSEDED in writing"
   - **Replacement:** "REG P2 and proposed P2c both prohibit this wording ("free assessment" / "free systems assessment"). Usable only if the CEO supersedes P2/P2c in writing, the paid stage never uses "assessment" alone, the R&C disclaimer is shown on the booking page and the summary, and no value is attached. R&C notes that "Growth" may imply an outcome (s.52(4))."
4. **§1 "Scope note" (final sentence).**
   - **Current:** "SIG only once D47 prerequisites exist."
   - **Replacement:** "Do not send the scope note until D47 prerequisites exist. Every scope note must include the s.6(2) identification and contact information (valid ≥60 days, s.6(3)) and an unsubscribe mechanism valid ≥60 days after sending (s.11(1)–(2)), even if it qualifies as transactional under s.6(6)."
   - **Basis:** LEGAL REQUIREMENT. s.6(6) disapplies only s.6(1)(a), so s.6(1)(b)/(2) still apply.
5. **§1 timing 0:00–2:00 (Opening).**
   - **Current:** "…ask consent for written notes (deletable on request); no recording without separate express permission; R&C warning…"
   - **Add, before asking for note consent:** "We'll take brief notes only to prepare your [FREE REVIEW NAME]; [name/title] can answer any questions about how we handle them [, and our video/booking tool is hosted outside Canada — our policy is at [URL]]."
   - **Basis:** LEGAL REQUIREMENT, PIPA s.13(1)(a)–(b) (purpose and contact person, "before or at the time of collecting") and s.13.1(3) where an outside-Canada provider is used. The booking-page notice doesn't reach prospects booked by referral or at events.
6. **§5 "Post-summary follow-up cadence".**
   - **Current:** "…Max 3 promotional follow-ups; any "no" (incl. verbal) → suppression list."
   - **Replacement:** "…Max 3 promotional follow-ups; check the suppression list immediately before every send (CHK S4) and record the result; each message carries s.6(2) content and an unsubscribe valid ≥60 days (s.11(2)); any "no" (incl. verbal) → suppression list the same day, actioned ≤10 business days (s.11(3)); never re-add a suppressed address (CHK S6)."
7. **§2 "Never" list (and CEO sheet K-9).**
   - **Current:** "health workflows (clinics held, D20 — front-office website review only, if the CEO allows, K-9)"
   - **Replacement:** "clinics and health-sector prospects: no free consultation or review until counsel confirms HIA/PIPA scope (D20; PIPA s.4(3)(f)). K-9 stays open for counsel."
   - **Why (INTERPRETATION):** even a front-office review means booking, calls and notes with a possible HIA custodian, and the prospect may volunteer health or registration information. The R&C GTM-32 position (R3) is "not eligible".
   - In the CEO sheet K-9, add "(R&C: not eligible pending counsel)" next to the Strategy option. It's already noted, so make it the default.
8. **§0 "Superseded stale paid-assessment text", row 2 (RECOMMENDATION, optional).**
