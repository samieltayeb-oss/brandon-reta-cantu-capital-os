## 3. Gaps the follow-up must fill
1. **A concrete first paid engagement (gap).** The deck ended with "pilot A or B, choose after discovery" and never named a NEXORA offer. Add the approved path: free 20-minute call → free NEXORA Business Growth & Technology Assessment (up to two workflows, a two-page findings report) → optional **paid Statement of Work — Discovery & Design** → a scoped implementation proposal → optional care. No prices; the CEO has to approve pricing (D01). Note: because HNH already completed the questionnaire, the CEO and Argon need to decide whether the free assessment has effectively been delivered and the next step is Discovery & Design directly (U).
2. **CaseEasy-centred differentiation (gap).** The deck treated CaseEasy as one of three advertised vendors. HNH now uses it (V per the CEO). Add a "build on CaseEasy, don't replace it" position: map its **publicly documented** capabilities (Strategy Analyst to verify on caseeasy.ca) against the gaps the deck already found: the website-to-intake handoff, consent and notice records, a claim register and reporting. Always phrase it as "CaseEasy documents X; whether HNH has it enabled is for Argon to confirm". Never claim an integration or API exists (U until Argon confirms).
3. **Evidence (gap).** The deck had no NEXORA delivery proof. Use only verifiable items: the method, the deliverable samples (synthetic), and the governance gates. No invented case studies or client claims.
4. **HNH-specific business value (gap).** The deck was organized around NEXORA's workstreams. Restructure around Zain's decisions: which one workflow, what success looks like, who owns it. Questionnaire-derived priorities may only come through Argon and CEO-approved summaries (U).
5. **A compelling next-meeting ask (gap).** The previous ask (the questionnaire) is fulfilled. The new ask: a 45–60 minute working session with Zain and the HNH operations owner to (a) confirm the findings readout and (b) choose one Discovery & Design scope. Approved materials only, sent by the CEO.
