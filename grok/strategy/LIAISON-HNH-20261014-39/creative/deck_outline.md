**Purpose:** a follow-up to the 15-slide "Preliminary / Proposed Discovery Briefing" that Zain has already seen (context/HNH_original_deck_15.pptx, 9 Oct 2026). > **Rules:**
> - No ROI, prices, customer, result or experience claims (GTM-31 register N31, N32, N24/N25, P3).
> - No outcome lines such as "entered once" (N6, N30).
> - Use the register terms: "offers a review of…", "CRM and sales follow-up", "accounting and operations integration", "with people in charge" (N7, N9, R7).

> - **Vendor claims not repeated:** CaseEasy's marketing claims ("500+ firms", SOC 2, encryption, "saves hours", "data hosted exclusively in Canada", the money-back guarantee, testimonials) are not repeated as fact.
