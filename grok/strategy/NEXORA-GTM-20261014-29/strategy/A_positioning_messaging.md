## 1. Positioning statement (final draft)
**NEXORA is the Connected Operations & Digital Transformation Partner for growing businesses in Calgary and Alberta.** We connect the systems you already run, automate the work in between with people in control, and build the digital experiences, CRM and custom software your next stage of growth needs. Then we keep it all running.
- Priority segment: 3–75 employees, Calgary/Alberta (priority, not an exclusive limit; see GTM-26 D28).
- Primary offer: outcome-focused engagements that grow into larger builds and recurring managed services.
- SummitOS: a vertical product for specialty exterior/roofing contractors. It is in a Controlled Paid Pilot, unsigned and not available for commitments. It is never the lead offer.

## 2. Messaging pillars
1. **Connected operations:** "Enter it once. See it everywhere." Integrations, data handoffs, dashboards.
2. **Governed automation and AI:** "Automation that asks before it acts." Human approval, limits, alerts (site: /ai-automation).
3. **Digital experiences that feed your systems:** websites and portals that send leads straight into CRM and operations.
4. **Build what doesn't exist:** custom software and integrations where off-the-shelf tools stop.
5. **A partner that stays:** managed care, monitoring and continuous improvement.

## 4. Proof-safe claims vs claims to avoid
**Safe (supportable today):**
- "Calgary-based team connecting websites, CRM, operations and accounting tools."
- "A person stays in charge of anything important" (published approach).
- "Founder with 20+ years in enterprise IT, cyber security, ERP and automation" (/about wording).
- "We work with the tools you already have where feasible" (/systems).
- "We label what's live, what's a pilot and what's a demo."
- "Fixed-scope discovery with agreed success measures" (offer design, once approved).
**Avoid until evidence exists:**
- Any results, ROI, hours-saved or client-count figures.
- "Proven", "trusted by", "leading" or similar superlatives.
- AI agents or autonomous operations; AEO/GEO or SEO retainers.
- SummitOS availability, signing, timelines, or modules not shown as delivered (e.g., Beacon/ABC/Convoy sync, payroll, Stripe/EFT; GTM-26 D05).
- WMS as a product for sale; ERP implementation (as opposed to integration).
- Prospect self-claims repeated externally (R&C §4).

## 6. Differentiation (vs established firms and low-cost sellers)
- **Breadth with integration at the centre:** one partner from website to CRM to operations to accounting. Competitors in GTM-26 03 mostly sell a single layer (web, SEO, HubSpot, single Zapier/Make workflows, or custom builds).
- **Senior experience at SMB scale:** enterprise ERP and cyber background, delivered directly.
- **Governance by design:** approval rules, monitoring and alerts. Fits owners wary of AI.
- **Extend, don't replace:** lower switching risk.
- **Transparent status and risk reversal:** fixed-scope discovery with agreed success criteria, plus a deliverable the client owns even if they stop there.
- Honest gap: no verified case study yet. First priority is a client-approved case study (GTM-26 D10).
