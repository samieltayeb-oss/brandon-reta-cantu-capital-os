## 1. Construction and trades
- **Angle:** each number entered once, from estimate to invoice.
- **Headline:** *Estimate once. Invoice right.*
- **Copy basis:** "We connect estimating, supplier pricing, work orders and invoicing so each number is entered once." (/industries)
- **Visual (A2):**
  - A charcoal side column with the mark crop and a vertical "Calgary, Alberta".
  - On the right, a hairline-ruled index: Estimate · Supplier pricing · Work order · Invoice.
  - Optional faint blueprint-grid hairlines, as in concept B1.
- **CTA:** Talk about your workflow.
- **Avoid:**
  - Presenting SummitOS as a product. If it appears at all, label it "Controlled Paid Pilot".
  - Job-loss claims, the site's demo or "Active Pilot" names, and prices.

## 2. Property management
- **Angle:** requests that don't get lost, reports that don't take hours.
- **Headline:** *Requests that don't get lost. Reports that don't take hours.*
- **Copy basis:** "We set up one place for requests, connect it to your email and calendar, and build simple reports. This is custom work, not a ready-made product." (/industries)
- **Visual (A2):** the speculative property concept layout (LIAISON-07) without the speculative label. The index reads Maintenance requests · Tenant & vendor details · Email & calendar · Owner reports.
- **CTA:** Talk about your requests and reports.
- **Avoid:** implying a property management product, which the site says NEXORA doesn't have, and any portal-vendor claims.

## 3. Home services (field service, HVAC, movers)
- **Angle:** from the first call to the finished job, without the retyping.
- **Headline:** *From first call to final invoice, one connected system.*
- **Copy basis:** customer tracking (CRM), workflow automation, customer communication and tool integrations (/capabilities).
- **Gap:** home services isn't a named industry on /industries, so the campaign uses only general service wording.
- **Visual (A2):** the side column, plus a calm index of Enquiry · Booking · Job · Follow-up, with the services listed in small caps beneath.
- **CTA:** Request a strategy call.
- **Avoid:** "SummitOS for home services", the "lead to invoice" phrasing that belongs to SummitOS, unsupported sector-specific claims, and dispatch or WMS promises.
