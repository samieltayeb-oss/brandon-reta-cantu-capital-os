## Positioning
NEXORA is a Calgary technology and business systems team (/capabilities). It is a full-service partner for websites, brand identity, CRM, workflow automation, reporting, customer communication and tool integrations, with transformation delivered in steps. SummitOS is one pilot within construction, not the headline.

## Hero
- **Headline:** Your business tools should work together. *(the site's own line, kept)*
- **Alternate:** One connected system, from first enquiry to final report.
- **Subhead:** NEXORA helps growing Calgary and Alberta businesses connect their tools, simplify daily work and automate repetitive tasks, with people in charge.
- **CTA:** Request a strategy call

## Proof-free value structure (three pillars, site wording)
1. **Connect your tools.** We link the software you already use so information is entered once.
2. **Simplify daily work.** We map how work really flows and remove the extra steps.
3. **Automate repetitive tasks.** Follow-ups, reminders and data entry run on their own, and a person stays in charge of anything important.

## Tone
Plain, calm and specific. Short sentences, "you" and "we". Practical over futuristic, with no AI hype. Concrete everyday problems instead of statistics. Editorial A2 styling: charcoal, restrained gold, Cormorant Garamond 500 headlines with Inter body.

## Avoid
Invented metrics, client logos, case results, "guaranteed", SummitOS or VITALIS presented as available products, and fake dashboards.
