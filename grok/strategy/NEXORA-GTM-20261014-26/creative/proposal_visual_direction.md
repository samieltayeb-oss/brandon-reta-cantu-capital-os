# B2B proposal visual direction (DRAFT)
> **DRAFT — NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** Internal go-to-market draft (NEXORA-GTM-20261014-26). No website files modified. All NEXORA wording is grounded in nexorayyc.io (homepage, /capabilities, /industries, re-read 2026-10-14). No clients, results, testimonials or awards. SummitOS and the warehouse tool appear only as **pilot or demonstration**, never as production-ready.

**System:** the A2 editorial language.
- **Colours:** deep charcoal #070707 page or cover, #121110 side column, ivory #F4F0E4 text, restrained gold #C9A646 for rules, numerals and italic accents.
- **Logo:** only the official crop of logo-primary.png (mark only, or mark plus wordmark), with no pixel changes, plus a typeset NEXORA wordmark in Inter Light with wide tracking.
- **Print variant:** ivory paper (#F4F0E4) with charcoal text and gold rules for long-form interior pages.

## Page types
1. **Cover.** Charcoal with the side column (mark, wordmark, vertical "Calgary, Alberta"). Title in Cormorant 500 at 64–72pt, the client's company as text only (no client logos without permission), date and the "Proposal · No." metadata in small caps.
2. **Contents.** A Roman-numeral, hairline-ruled index like the services flyer.
3. **Understanding.** "What we heard". Discovery findings, quoted or paraphrased, with hypotheses clearly marked as such.
4. **Approach.** Learn → Plan and build → Improve, as three columns with gold numerals.
5. **Scope.** A table of services in scope, each linked to a /capabilities service name.
6. **Timeline.** Hairline phases on a grid, no Gantt clutter.
7. **Investment.** A simple ruled table. Prices are filled in per proposal after approval; none are in this template.
8. **Working with us.** Automation with people in charge; who decides what.
9. **Next steps / sign-off.** Fixed fields, with no marketing.

## Type scale (Cormorant Garamond 500 / Inter)
| Role | Font | Size | Leading | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display (cover) | Cormorant 500 | 72pt | 1.0 | −1% |
| H1 | Cormorant 500 | 40pt | 1.05 | −0.5% |
| H2 | Cormorant 500 italic, gold | 26pt | 1.15 | 0 |
| Body | Inter 300 | 10.5pt | 1.55 | 0 |
| Table / UI | Inter 400 | 9.5pt | 1.4 | 0 |
| Labels / small caps | Inter 500 caps | 8pt | 1.3 | +30% |
| Numerals (index) | Inter 500 | 8pt, gold | — | +20% |

**Grid:** A4 or Letter with a 12-column grid. The side column is 3 columns wide on covers and section openers. Margins are 18mm, with generous negative space.

**Note:** the brand fonts verified on the site are Cormorant Garamond, DM Sans and Space Mono (LIAISON-04 typography check). Inter is used here because it was approved in A2. Consider switching to DM Sans for strict site consistency.
