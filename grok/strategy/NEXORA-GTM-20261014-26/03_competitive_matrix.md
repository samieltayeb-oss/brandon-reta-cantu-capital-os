## D. Competitive readout (HYPOTHESES)
1. **Calgary SMB price anchors are low and public.** Single-workflow automation is advertised at $1,500–$2,250 (Oxone, Seal Grow, NuTab); websites at $499–$1,500; SEO/AEO at $499–$895/mo. NEXORA publishes no service prices (01), so buyers compare against these anchors anyway.
2. **AEO/"AI search" is already productized locally** (BrainWage $599/mo, internetWorld, Ember North). NEXORA has no such page — a gap, not a differentiator.
3. **Differentiation room** = cross-system integration + governance (human approval, audit) + founder's enterprise ERP/cyber background. Most low-price competitors sell single Zapier/Make workflows; Eikeland and Tactics compete on custom builds.
4. **SummitOS vs SaaS:** Jobber's published USD plans span the SummitOS CAD $299–599 band; AccuLynx/Buildertrend/Procore hide price. SummitOS's supplier-price-impact feature is its stated wedge; parity on breadth is not credible in pilot.
5. **WMS:** Zoho Inventory starts at C$39/mo → a NEXORA WMS product (DEMO) faces a cheap incumbent floor; integration services are the safer play.
