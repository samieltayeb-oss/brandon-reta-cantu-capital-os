## Why choose NEXORA over an established firm (honest version)
1. **Integration across systems, not one tool:** many local competitors sell single workflows or a single platform (HubSpot partners, web shops; 03). NEXORA's offer is connecting website, CRM, operations and accounting.
2. **Senior, enterprise-grade experience at SMB scale:** founder background in ERP, cyber security and automation (/about). The buyer works directly with that experience.
3. **Governed automation:** approval rules, failure alerts and "a person stays in charge" are published commitments (/ai-automation, /capabilities/autonomous-operations). These are useful for owners wary of AI.
4. **Extend, don't replace:** NEXORA works with incumbent tools (e.g., Rent Manager; the Ridgewalk configurator and WordPress/WooCommerce site at Core Fabrication), which lowers switching risk.
5. **Transparent status:** the site labels demos, pilots and unverified work. Turn this into a trust message: "we'll tell you what's real".
6. **Risk reversal instead of case studies (proposal):** a fixed-scope paid assessment with a clear deliverable, milestone billing and success criteria agreed up front. Price is to be set by the CEO.
Avoid claims like "proven results", "hundreds of clients" or ROI numbers until a client approves a verified case study.
