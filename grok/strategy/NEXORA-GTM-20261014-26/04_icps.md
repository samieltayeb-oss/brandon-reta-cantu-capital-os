## ICP 1 — Multi-site field-service & trades operator ("intake-to-invoice")
- **Firmographics:** Calgary/Alberta, 3–75 employees (CEO standing target; the website's /industries page states "about 15 to 150" for construction — widening is CEO decision D28); 2+ sites or crews; owner-led. Sectors: electrical, HVAC/home services, movers, exterior/roofing.
- **Evidence:** Colz Electric — 2 sites (T1), 24/7 dispatch (self-stated), site relaunch 2026-08-04 (T3); score 50 (Sprint 03). Transformer Exteriors — 2 open T2 postings listing estimating and scheduling (62). Personal Movers / Action Furnace (T3, weaker or stale). 02: home services and trades tie at 24/35.
- **Problem (HYPOTHESIS, general industry):** call/web intake → dispatch → invoicing re-keyed across tools; estimating and crew scheduling manual.
- **Trigger events to watch:** new site, hiring for estimators/dispatch (alis postings), acquisition, website relaunch.
- **Best offers (05):** Connected-Systems Assessment → Lead-to-Job Automation Sprint → Managed Care. SummitOS only for roofing/exterior, as pilot/design-partner, with CEO approval.
- **Disqualifiers:** already fully on an all-in-one FSM (e.g., Jobber) with no integration gap; owner-operator size (Sidhu/Vami-type).
- **Competition:** Jobber self-serve (USD, published); solo automation sellers from $1,500 (Oxone, Seal Grow) — 03.
- **Channel-fit note for Sales Strategist (HYPOTHESES):** (1) Trigger-based, research-led contact through the company's public business channel after CEO approval — triggers are public and dated (alis postings, registry licences). (2) Search/AEO content on "dispatch", "quote follow-up" problems, since competitors already capture this search intent with published prices (03). (3) Trade/supplier associations and local business groups for referral credibility. Why: owner buyers, short cycles, price-anchored market → proof + clear fixed-scope entry offer matter more than brand.

## ICP 2 — Property & facilities manager extending an incumbent platform
- **Firmographics:** Calgary/Alberta property or condo managers, 3–75 employees (CEO standing target), running a PM platform (e.g., Rent Manager). Firms near the lower end (3–10) may lack platform API access (H).
- **Evidence:** AspirePeak — condo division 2026-06-16 (T3 + T1 echo), Rent Manager portals (T1), score 65 (#3 Sprint 03). Rimrock — 5-brokerage split (2024, stale). NEXORA lists "Property and facilities" as a target (site). 02: property management 24/35.
- **Problem (HYPOTHESIS):** work-order intake, owner/board communications and reporting outside the platform's native features.
- **Best offers:** Assessment → Automation Sprint built on the incumbent (no replacement) → Managed Care (strong recurring fit).
- **Disqualifiers:** platform already covers the need natively; no API/export access.
- **Competition:** PM platform vendors' own add-ons (not priced — gap); general automation agencies.
- **Channel-fit note (HYPOTHESES):** (1) Platform-ecosystem channel — partner/marketplace listings or user communities for the PM platforms, since the incumbent defines the integration surface. (2) Industry associations for condo/property managers (not yet researched — gap). (3) Content showing "extend, don't replace" with governance (human approval, audit). Why: buyers fear platform migration; credibility comes from knowing their platform.

## ICP 3 — Growing multi-site producer or distributor needing connected operations
- **Firmographics:** 3–75-employee fabrication, light manufacturing or distribution firms in the Calgary region with an e-commerce/order front end plus shop/accounting/inventory systems.
- **Evidence:** Core Fabrication — 13k→24k sq ft move (T1, 2026-05-20; stale 2026-11-16), Ridgewalk Software configurator embedded as an externally hosted iframe (core.ridgewalksoftware.com) on its WordPress/WooCommerce site (GROK-24 digital_audit.md) (T1), score 76 (#1). EGM Drywall — second office (T1, 2026-08-11), 71, Edmonton FLAG. A.T.W. (stale, FLAG). 02: manufacturing 20/35, logistics 18/35 (targets often too large or already on a WMS).
- **Problem (HYPOTHESIS):** orders/quotes re-keyed into work orders, scheduling and accounting; multi-office job-costing/reporting.
- **Best offers:** paid discovery → Connected Operations Build (milestones) → Managed Care. Not NEXORA WMS (DEMO; Zoho Inventory from C$39/mo, 03).
- **Disqualifiers:** central buying outside Alberta; existing ERP integrator; above the 3–75 band. Note: multi-site producers/distributors with ERP needs may sit above 75 (EGM size flagged UNKNOWN, possibly larger) — whether to widen this ICP's band is CEO decision D28.
- **Competition:** custom builders (Eikeland, Tactics, Pixeltree — 03), ERP resellers (not researched — gap).
- **Channel-fit note (HYPOTHESES):** (1) Founder-led, account-based approach using the CEO's enterprise ERP/integration background (/about) — higher deal values justify per-account research. (2) Manufacturing/industrial associations and economic-development networks (not yet researched — gap). (3) Case-study content once a delivered integration is verified — currently none (01). Why: longer, higher-trust cycles; proof and technical credibility outweigh price.
