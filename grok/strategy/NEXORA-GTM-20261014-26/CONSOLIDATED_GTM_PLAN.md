## 0. The strategy in one page
1. **Position** NEXORA as a *connected-operations partner* for Calgary/Alberta firms of **3–75 employees**: connect the tools they already use, automate handoffs with a person approving, keep it running (H; recommended by Strategy Analyst, CEO decision D02).

4. **Target three ICPs** — multi-site trades/field service, property managers on an incumbent platform, growing producers/distributors — via a short, CEO-approved account list, founder networking and two Calgary Chamber events; content/SEO in parallel.
5. **Gate everything** behind the 11-item pre-outreach compliance checklist; **SUMMITOS** is a controlled paid pilot, mentioned only as design-partner discovery with CEO approval.
6. **Measure weekly** with a one-page RAG dashboard from Command Center's existing CRM (if approved).
