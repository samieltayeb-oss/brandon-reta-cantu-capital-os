## 3. nexorayyc.io as a sales-conversion system (assessment)
Strengths (verified on page):
- One primary CTA repeated ("Request a strategy call") plus email fallback (growth@nexorayyc.io).
- Clear problem framing ("The same data, typed again and again").
- Honesty signals: status badges on products and case studies ("Needs Delivery Verification", "Concept Demonstration"); "we only share results our clients have approved". Strong trust posture for B2B.
- Six ungated checklists matched to sectors and problems (good top-of-funnel and SEO assets).
Weaknesses / conversion leaks (verified unless marked H):
1. **Thin proof.** /work: 3 client items marked "Needs Delivery Verification" (QTSI, Tech-Micro, Seha), 1 "Active Pilot" (Blue Moon), 7 "Concept Demonstration", 1 internal. No integration/automation case study; all real items are websites/brands. Biggest gap for a "connected systems" seller.
2. **Diluted portfolio signal.** Consumer demos (trip planner, relocation guide, driver exam, creator dashboard) sit beside B2B offers on /products. H: this lowers perceived focus for an operations buyer.
3. **SummitOS prominence.** It's the only product on the homepage hero band; with "don't treat SummitOS as main business", it over-weights construction.
4. **No lead capture on checklists** ("no email needed"): good for trust, but zero conversion data. Deliberate trade-off — CEO decision.
5. **No service pricing or engagement entry point** (no "assessment from…" or fixed-scope first step). Competitors publish prices (see 03).
6. **Phone in schema is +1-438** (a Montréal-area code) for a Calgary firm (homepage JSON-LD). H: minor local-trust friction.
7. **No AEO/GEO/SEO or marketing-automation service pages** despite strong market demand visible in competitor pages (03).
8. Draft legal docs for SummitOS.
