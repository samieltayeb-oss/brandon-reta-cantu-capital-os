**Method:** Public sources only. Rank = Florida SBA 7(a)/504 volume + Florida-specific demand drivers (storm rebuild, insurance/cash-flow pressure, tourism seasonality, equipment intensity) + fit to $25K–$500K products (incl. MCA/alt). Labels: **VERIFIED** (primary public dataset or statute), **REPORTED** (reputable secondary aggregating primary data), **INFERENCE** (analyst synthesis of cited evidence).  
**Hard limits observed:** No Google Maps / site scraping; no named business lead lists or contacts; no PII; no paid enrichment/Apollo; no outreach.

---

## Ranking caveat (read first)

“Highest-converting” is **not** a measured conversion rate for Bank Breezy. It is a **proxy rank** from (1) Florida SBA approval counts/dollars by NAICS, (2) FL SBCS financing-need patterns, (3) ELFA equipment-finance intensity where relevant, and (4) Florida macro drivers. Past SBA approvals ≠ future approvals or MCA conversion. High historical charge-off industries (local trucking, remodelers) may convert more often but perform worse for the funder—treat as risk-adjusted targeting, not a green light.

## Cross-cutting public buying signals (all categories)

Use only **public** lookups; do not scrape Maps results into lead lists without a separate, authorized process.

1. **Florida Secured Transaction Registry (UCC)** — https://floridaucc.com/search — new equipment/vehicle liens.  
2. **Sunbiz / Division of Corporations** — entity formation, amendments, FEI/EIN notices as filed.

## Compliance & product caveats (must brief client)

1. **Florida Commercial Financing Disclosure Law (effective transactions on/after 2024-01-01)** — Fla. Stat. Part of ch. 559 (§§ 559.9611 et seq.). Providers generally must give written disclosures (amount provided, amount disbursed, total repay, dollar cost, payment schedule/methodology, prepayment). **>$500,000 transactions exempt**; **≤5 FL transactions / 12 months** exempt; banks/credit unions broadly carved out; AG exclusive enforcement (fines). Bank Breezy / any MCA or commercial finance offer in FL must be compliance-reviewed—**this research is not legal advice**.  
2. **Ticket filter:** Child care, dental, physicians, and hotels often clear **above $500K** (disclosure exemption threshold and product band). Engine should cap/filter to $25K–$500K.
