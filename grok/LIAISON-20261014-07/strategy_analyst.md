## 1. Edon Management / Edon Properties Inc.
**(a) Relevance: High.** This is the only company in the set with a PRIMARY-source, named "Digital Transformation program" and named owners: a VP Corporate Strategy & Technology, and a VP Finance & IT who is responsible for client reporting. The HQ is in Calgary and the company runs several service lines.
**(c) Confirmed facts** (from Market Scout):
- Calgary HQ; President since incorporation in 1992. https://edonmgmt.com/our-team/ (PRIMARY, seen 2026-10-14)
- The VP Corporate Strategy & Technology leads the "Digital Transformation program". Same URL, seen 2026-10-14. The program's start date and scope are UNKNOWN because the page is undated.
- The VP Finance & IT owns IT systems and client reporting. Same URL, seen 2026-10-14.
- Service lines: property management, trades, caretaking, grounds and security across Alberta. https://edonmgmt.com/careers-find-your-next-team/ (PRIMARY, seen 2026-10-14)
- Unverified: "1.2M m²" and "~320 staff" (LinkedIn SNIPPET_ONLY, seen 2026-10-14).
**GENERAL industry challenges (not confirmed for Edon):** Property and facilities firms that run several lines often split work orders, accounting, payroll and timekeeping, and client reporting across separate systems. Client reporting is often assembled by hand in spreadsheets, and field crews may capture work on paper or in mobile apps that aren't integrated.
**(b) Competitive alternatives:** platforms and their partner networks for property management and CMMS/work orders (for example the Yardi, MRI or ServiceTitan class, and CMMS vendors); Microsoft Dynamics, Sage or NetSuite resellers; Edon's own IT under the VP Finance & IT; national or regional consultancies doing digital transformation; Calgary IT managed-service providers.
**(d) Best-fit service: Connected systems.** The documented owner is responsible for "client reporting" and for IT systems that "enable team members". Integrating the finance, operations and reporting tools Edon already has is a smaller, less disruptive starting point than a wholesale transformation engagement, and it fits a program that is already underway. AI workflow automation could follow later. SummitOS and WMS don't fit.
**(e) Recommendation: YES, investigate further (priority 1).** Next public checks: when the program started and what it covers, any published vendors or case studies, and current job postings mentioning systems.

## 2. MODUS Structures Inc.
**(a) Relevance: Medium-High.** A PRIMARY release confirms a two-company merger under one brand across 3 facilities with 460+ employees. A job ad mentions integrating "financial data for multiple projects". The head office is in Crossfield, close to Calgary.
**(c) Confirmed facts:**
- Acquisition of ROC Modular completed: 460+ employees, about 260,000 sq ft, 3 facilities, operating under the MODUS brand. https://modus.ca/projects-news/modus-acquires-roc-modular/ (PRIMARY, 2026-09-09)
- "sharing production knowledge and technology across its facilities". Same URL, 2026-09-09.
- A Project Manager posting says the role "Integrates financial data for multiple projects" and follows up on sales leads. https://www.jobs.ca/modus-structures-inc/jobs/project-manager-crossfield-ab-93dc3793431f (SECONDARY, posted 2026-10-11)
**GENERAL industry challenges (not confirmed for MODUS):** After a manufacturing merger, firms commonly run two ERPs or accounting systems, two sets of estimating and BOM data, and two different ways of handling project costs. That makes it hard to report consistent job margins and to schedule production across plants. Modular builders also have to connect design and BOM, procurement, plant scheduling and site installation.
**(b) Competitive alternatives:** manufacturing ERP vendors and their resellers (the Epicor, SAP Business One, Dynamics 365 or Acumatica class), construction ERP (the Sage Intacct Construction, Procore or Viewpoint class), MODUS's own IT and finance teams, systems integrators that work with the acquiring company or its advisers, and offshore development shops.
**(d) Best-fit service: Connected systems.** The release talks about sharing technology across facilities, and the job ad mentions integrating project financial data. Both point to consolidating data and integrating systems rather than building new software. SummitOS is aimed at roofing and exterior contractors and doesn't fit a modular manufacturer, so I'm not pushing it here.
**(e) Recommendation: YES, investigate further (priority 2).** The merger closed recently (2026-09-09), so decisions about the systems may still be open. Next public checks: ERP or tool names in job ads, and whether ROC's systems are being retired.
