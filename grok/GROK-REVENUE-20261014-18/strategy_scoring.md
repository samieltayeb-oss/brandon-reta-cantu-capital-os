## Scoring rubric (how points were awarded)
- **Trigger (25):** dated growth event. T1/T2 dated = 18–23; T3 dated = 10–15; month only or weak (partner hire, relocation) = 5–9; undated = 3.
- **Ops opportunity (20):** only an operational change the source states (new site, bigger shop, new division, merger) counts. A hypothesis alone earns ≤4.
- **Capability match (20):** how well a NEXORA capability fits the stated operating model. A partner or competitor profile scores low.
- **Purchasing capacity (15):** a disclosed raise or a stated facility investment only. Otherwise 0 U.
- **Channel (10):** a verified business website from the ledger check = 10; title or ownership unconfirmed = 5. Website only; no contacts.
- **Evidence quality/freshness (10):** tier plus age (T1/T2 within 6 months scores highest).

Capability codes: AI = AI workflow automation · CRM = CRM/lead mgmt · ERP = ERP/ops integration · WMS = WMS/inventory · SUM = SUMMITOS · WEB = premium website/digital transformation · MKT = marketing/lead-gen · SaaS = custom SaaS/product engineering.
Classifications: **V** = VERIFIED FIT SIGNAL (the cited ledger source states the underlying operational fact) · **H** = HYPOTHESIS · **NA** = NOT_ASSESSED · **X** = EVIDENCED_NOT_A_FIT.
Note: a "V" means only that the source shows the operational condition (e.g. two sites). It does not mean the company has a stated need or intent to buy.
